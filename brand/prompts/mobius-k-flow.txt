Design an exquisite original K-shaped Mobius ribbon logomark for Kodo. Deliver only ONE logo symbol on a pure warm-white background. This is a brand identity mark: simple, poised, memorable, beautifully curved.

The defining idea is a continuous one-sided ribbon loop folded into the recognizable silhouette of an upright capital K. LEFT: a broad, nearly vertical elongated curved ribbon spine. CENTER: one smooth half-twist, a flowing waist with a small clean negative-space opening. RIGHT: two sweeping ribbon limbs, one rising diagonally toward the upper right, the other dropping toward the lower right. Their tips curl back into the hidden reverse side of the continuous loop. The outer silhouette must be K, not R, B, X, an infinity symbol or a bow. The left spine is visibly integrated with the right arms, no disconnected stem.

Make the curves the hero: long precise cubic-Bezier sweeps, generous radii, natural thick-to-thin perspective, unbroken smooth tangent transitions. Sculpt the negative spaces as carefully as the ribbon. Use a restrained number of contours. The mark must feel like a single beautifully cut ribbon with one effortless twist, not several assembled pieces. Sympathetic optical balance and soft tailored ends. Elegant contemporary Swiss identity sensibility with the grace of a folded silk band.

Color: deep periwinkle-cobalt blue with very subtle lighter blue on the reverse surface and deeper indigo only inside the twist. Predominantly a clear cobalt blue; no green anywhere. Prefer a nearly flat graphic surface with only restrained tonal shading to explain the twist. NO glossy 3D rendering, no physical material texture, no thickness extrusion, no bevel, no chrome, no glow, no lens effects, no dramatic lighting, no drop shadow. Smooth clean antialiased curves, large uncomplicated masses and sharply controlled contours.

Centered, upright, front-facing. Mark around 64 percent of canvas height, generous balanced margins. Plain #FAF9F6 background. No text, no typography, no name, no tagline, no design-board layout, no icon tile, no frame, no extra objects, no watermark. A highly polished final logo presentation, not a sketch.
