Design an original abstract ribbon monogram for Kodo that feels LIGHT, ALIVE and effortlessly elegant. Use a Mobius-like continuous band and a faint lowercase-k gesture, but the letter must be suggested rather than constructed. Prioritize sculptural rhythm and beautiful negative space over typographic correctness.

Start from a loose handwritten flourish: one broad sweeping curve curls up and back into a small open loop on the upper LEFT, dives diagonally through a beautifully controlled half-twist, and springs outward to the RIGHT in one long, softly lifted ribbon wing. A much shorter curved return sweeps downward and curls inward near the base. The result has an irregular, jaunty compact silhouette, as if a silky band is gently dancing or a calligraphic brushstroke has become a continuous folded loop. Let the two flowing rightward gestures and the left curl only vaguely evoke k.

CRITICAL: absolutely no vertical straight bar, no conventional uppercase K, no upright linear spine, no mirrored upper/lower branches, no literal alphabet glyph, no stacked equal loops, no rigid three-pronged construction. One side higher, the other side shorter; subtly off-axis, gently leaning forward. Use deliberate asymmetry, broad-to-narrow curve tension, soft organic roundness and a little buoyant bounce. Leave generous airy negative space. Do not add lots of twists: one main Mobius half-turn is enough. The silhouette should stay simple, bold and memorable. Gracefully playful, not childish or comic.

Exquisite long continuous Bezier-like curves and smooth surfaces. A clean minimal brand-design finish with subtle satin shading only where the band twists. Cobalt and periwinkle blue with a gentle blue-violet underside; the mark should feel bright and inviting. No green or teal anywhere. No chrome, plastic gloss, glass, glow, dramatic lighting, hard bevels, thick extrusion, gritty texture or cast shadow. A folded thin sheet, not a rounded rope or an inflated balloon.

Only ONE large centered abstract logo symbol on a pure warm-white #FAF9F6 background, with generous clear margins. Front-facing logo presentation, about 60 percent canvas height. No typography, no wordmark, no labels, no design-board layout, no frame, no icon tile, no mockup, no watermark, no extra objects. Do not draw a generic infinity sign, chain link, butterfly or heart. The final impression is an agile, friendly, sophisticated ribbon gesture with a hidden k.
