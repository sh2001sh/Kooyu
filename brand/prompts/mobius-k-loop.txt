Create ONE exceptional premium brand logomark: a CLOSED MOBIUS RIBBON LOOP whose overall silhouette is unmistakably an upright capital letter K. Original brand identity for Kodo. The core challenge is to combine the elegance of a Mobius ring with the legibility of K.

The previous common ribbon-K approach is wrong for this task: do NOT just place a vertical bar next to two open flowing ribbons. Do NOT leave straight cut-off ribbon ends. The loop and its return path must be visibly recognizable.

Imagine one broad, thin, continuous blue satin band folded into a K-shaped sculptural loop. A tall, slim rounded vertical LOOP forms the left spine, with a narrow elongated internal aperture. From its middle, the band makes one graceful half-twist and flows out toward upper right, curls smoothly back around, sweeps through the central junction and out toward lower right, then curls back to complete the same continuous loop. Each apparent arm has a visible return curve; there are NO exposed ribbon ends. Use subtle front/back overlap at the central waist so the eye can trace a continuous path. The left spine stays upright, the right limbs clearly diverge diagonally in a K, with a generous wedge of open negative space between them. The outer silhouette must read K before the viewer notices the folded loop construction. Avoid an infinity sign, X, B, butterfly, bowtie, heart, chain link, or a complex Celtic knot. Do not create several intertwined rings.

Extremely graceful long Bezier curves, polished G2-like smooth turns, natural width variation from the twist, beautifully balanced apertures. Only one central twist, no excessive ripples. Simple strong shape, composed and generous, not ornamental. The loop must feel weightless and effortless. Thin folded sheet edges, not thick inflated tubing or an extruded solid.

Use predominantly rich cobalt blue #3659E3, a softly illuminated periwinkle reverse face, and deep ultramarine in the fold. Subtle controlled tonal transitions to describe the surface. No green, teal or turquoise. No rainbow. No glass, chrome, metallic specularity, glitter, glow, hard bevels, plastic or complicated texture. Almost front-facing orthographic view, not a dramatic perspective render. No floor or cast shadow.

ONE centered large logo symbol on pure warm white #FAF9F6. Logo occupies about 65 percent of the square canvas height. Generous clear margins. No typography, no wordmark, no labels, no captions, no border, no app-icon tile, no mockup, no watermark. An immaculate final brand design presentation.
